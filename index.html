Method & limits

  • Peg data: DefiLlama stablecoin prices (aggregated), sampled every 15 min. On-chain pool reads (Curve, Uniswap V3, PancakeSwap V3, Camelot V3) are being added.
  • "Re-pegged" = price back within 20 bps of $1. Time resolution = sampling interval.
  • Historical base rates: 262 depeg episodes since 2021 (daily data; survivorship bias: only stablecoins still listed).
  • Market regime: 4-state hidden Markov model on 11 years of public market data. It describes the current state; it does not predict prices.
  • Public alerts are delayed. Nothing here is financial advice.
